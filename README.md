# how-to-make-Rose-Scone
# 🌹 Rose Scones

Soft, buttery scones with a subtle rose flavor — perfect served with clotted cream and jam for a traditional British tea.

---

## 🕒 Preparation Time
- **Prep:** 15 minutes  
- **Bake:** 12–15 minutes  
- **Serves:** 8 scones  

---

## 🧂 Ingredients
- 250g (2 cups) all-purpose flour  
- 2 teaspoons baking powder  
- 50g (1/4 cup) sugar  
- 1/4 teaspoon salt  
- 60g (1/4 cup) cold unsalted butter, cubed  
- 150ml (2/3 cup) milk  
- 1 large egg  
- 1 teaspoon rose water  
- *Optional:* dried rose petals for garnish  
- Extra milk for brushing  

---

## 🔪 Instructions

1. **Preheat the oven**  
   - Set the oven to **200°C (400°F)**.  
   - Line a baking tray with parchment paper.

2. **Mix dry ingredients**  
   - In a large bowl, combine **flour**, **baking powder**, **sugar**, and **salt**.  
   - Rub in **cold butter cubes** with fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs.

3. **Add wet ingredients**  
   - In a small bowl, whisk together **milk**, **egg**, and **rose water**.  
   - Pour into the dry mixture and gently mix to form a soft dough.  
   - **Do not overmix**, or scones will be tough.

4. **Shape the scones**  
   - Lightly flour a surface and roll the dough to about **2 cm (3/4 inch)** thickness.  
   - Cut into rounds or wedges and place on the prepared tray.  
   - Optional: sprinkle **dried rose petals** on top for decoration.

5. **Brush and bake**  
   - Brush the tops lightly with **milk**.  
   - Bake for **12–15 minutes**, until risen and lightly golden.

6. **Serve**  
   - Cool slightly and serve warm with **clotted cream**, **butter**, or **jam**.

---

## 💡 Tips
- Keep **butter cold** for flaky, tender scones.  
- Use **rose water sparingly** — a little goes a long way.  
- Scones are best eaten the same day but can be lightly toasted the next day.  
- For a sweeter touch, add a teaspoon of **caster sugar** on top before baking.
